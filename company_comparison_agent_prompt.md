==================================================
4. DEFINE THE PURPOSE OF COMPANY COMPARISON
==================================================

Company Comparison should allow DealSense to compare multiple companies using the intelligence already generated by the platform.

The purpose is to answer:

"Which of these companies is stronger from an investment/due-diligence perspective, and why?"

Example:

Company A: Notion
Company B: Asana
Company C: ClickUp

The comparison should NOT merely compare names or produce a generic LLM paragraph.

It should compare structured investment-relevant dimensions.

Potential dimensions include:

- company overview
- industry
- business model
- products
- customers
- target market
- SaaS status
- B2B/B2C
- recurring revenue
- subscription model
- enterprise focus
- global presence
- AI positioning
- marketplace characteristics
- acquisition score
- recommendation
- major risks
- strengths
- weaknesses
- opportunities
- threats
- relevant recent news
- evidence-backed investment factors

Use only dimensions that are actually supported by the existing data models.

DO NOT invent data.
